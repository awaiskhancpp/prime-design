Why does Yelp recommend reviews?

Hundreds of millions of reviews are posted on Yelp, and our automated recommendation software works in the background to showcase the reviews that it determines are most helpful. It analyzes billions of data points from all reviews, reviewers, and businesses to evaluate the usefulness and reliability of each review—applying uniform rules to every business, whether or not it advertises on Yelp.

Great Yelp reviews and ratings should come from consumers who had authentic experiences that they’re inspired to share. Learn more here.
